What exactly is ASD Forge?

A business starter workspace for freelancers and independent service sellers. You can create offers, share service pages, track clients, and generate invoices.

Is it free to use?

Yes. This starter version is free to use. There are no automatic payments or subscription charges built into the workspace.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Everything is operated through simple forms and pages.

Are my account and business records stored in the cloud?

Not yet. Your workspace account and business drafts are saved on this device, without cross-device sign-in or password recovery. Public listings and order requests use a separate server. Export backups from Settings.

How can I contact a customer?

Buyers can submit a service request through your public listing. In your workspace, open Orders & Delivery → Customer Requests to view their email and any WhatsApp number they agreed to share. Contact opens your email or WhatsApp app; messages are not sent automatically.

Can customers pay me through Forge?

Not yet. Secure payment and refund code is being tested, but live card collection, protected settlement, and refunds are disabled until a licensed payment provider and the required approvals are in place.
